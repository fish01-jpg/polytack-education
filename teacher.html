Class settings (enforced for everyone who joins)

Students cannot change Fun Mode (or any class setting) from the student app — it only reads settings. Students can also pick any classmate in "Race a classmate" (their ghost car appears physically on the track), quiz results show via the 📊 Results button, and Solo practice (no join code) stays free and unrecorded.
Car physics (class default)
The baseline for every student's car — this really changes the car in the game (engine force, wheel grip, drag/top speed and braking). 1 = the game's normal car. Students' Garage performance upgrades stack on top of these. Changes apply live, even mid-race.
These settings override anything on the student side — students can't change quiz behavior.

Tracks mode settings

In Tracks mode the total questions are split across the tracks. Students answer a track's questions to unlock it, get a limited number of race attempts for their best score, then either re-unlock the same track (same questions) or move to the next track.

Garage & points

How the Garage works (painted cars, upgrades, powers, export/import)
Students earn points for correct answers and completed tracks, then spend them in the Garage: car paints (applied LIVE — no reload), performance upgrades, abilities and cosmetics. Each Garage upgrade is now ONE button — click it repeatedly to keep leveling up. 🚀 Jump Jets: SPACE pops the car off the ground (5 levels). ⚡ Jet Turbo: hold SHIFT for a sustained engine boost — the fuel tank refills every round. Once an upgrade is owned, a 🎚️ slider lets the student tune its power, and ♻️ Reset sells all performance upgrades back for a full refund. Students can 📤 Export their car as a code and 📥 Import it in Solo practice — classes never import, so the class car always comes from your class settings.
Run bank (attempts)

🎛️ Advanced physics tuning (student Garage)

When ON, students see advanced physics sliders in the Garage (mass, weight distribution, downforce, suspension stiffness/damping, steering speed, turning stability, drift momentum, air gravity). Changes apply LIVE. When OFF, the section is hidden and any saved physTune is ignored (not deleted) until you turn it back on.
Per-parameter range overrides — leave blank to use the built-in default range. Both min and max must be set to override a range.

⭐ Custom tracks (extra quiz tracks for all students — work in every mode)

How custom tracks work
Add a named track with its own questions. In tracks mode it appears AFTER the numbered tracks (students race the game's highest track as a stand-in); in all other modes its questions are mixed into the quiz pool.

🎮 Game tracks (raceable in the game itself)

Paste PolyTrack track import codes — one per line — and every student in this class gets them in their game's Play → track list automatically (they appear as the game's custom tracks). Get codes from the game's Editor (Export button). Students can race these tracks in any mode; runs record like any other run.

Question bank

JSON fields: question, choices (2–4), answer (zero-based index or exact choice text), explanation, category. CSV headers: question,a,b,c,d,answer,explanation,category.
